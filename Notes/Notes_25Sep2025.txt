## Clarifying the Terms

* **AI (Artificial Intelligence):** Broad umbrella term for systems that mimic human-like intelligence, from decision-making to perception.
* **ML (Machine Learning):** A subset of AI focused on algorithms that learn from data.
* **GenAI (Generative AI):** A subset of ML/AI that focuses on creating new content (text, images, music, code, etc.).

One correction:

* **GPT** stands for **“Generative Pre-trained Transformer”**, not *“Generative Preprocessor Text”*. The "Transformer" is the architecture behind it.

## Dimensions / Capabilities in AI

You listed some important ones. Here’s a more complete picture, grouped into categories:

### 1. **Language and Text**

* Text generation (stories, articles, chat)
* Summarization
* Translation
* Question answering
* Code assistance (coding copilots, debugging tools)

### 2. **Vision**

* Image generation (DALL·E, Stable Diffusion)
* Image recognition/classification (object detection, facial recognition)
* Text-to-image and image-to-text (captioning, scene understanding)
* Video generation and editing

### 3. **Speech and Audio**

* Text-to-speech (synthetic voices, voice cloning)
* Speech-to-text (transcription, voice assistants)
* Music generation and sound synthesis

### 4. **Multimodal AI**

* Models that combine text, vision, and audio (e.g., describe an image, generate images from prompts, answer questions about videos).

### 5. **Reasoning and Decision Support**

* Planning and problem solving
* Recommendation systems
* Autonomous agents (AI assistants, robotics)

### 6. **Robotics and Control**

* Computer vision for navigation
* Reinforcement learning for motion and interaction
* Industrial automation
